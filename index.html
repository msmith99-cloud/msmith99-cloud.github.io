Yankees Baseball - The Whole 9 Innings

Welcome! This website explains the history of the New York Yankees professional baseball team. It includes all-star player Aaron Judge, among other Yankees legends, and a brief trivia quiz.

Project

  • Project One — Passion for Pinstripes is a website focused on sharing information about baseball, but specifically the New York Yankees and their many star players. This will be a multi-page website that will consist of informational facts of Yankees baseball, links to official websites, images that represent the team and its players like Babe Ruth and Mickey Mantle, and an opportunity for Yankees trivia to test your knowledge. To create, HTML will be used to build a working website with responsive designs, such as accessibility for mobile, tablet, and desktop users.
