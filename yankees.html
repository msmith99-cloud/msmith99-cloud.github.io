The New York Yankees

The Legendary History of the Team

The New York Yankees baseball team are based in the Bronx in New York City. They are highly regarded as one of the most famous teams in MLB, considering they have won 27 World Series titles. Generations of Yankees players are regarded as well-known all-stars, such as Babe Ruth, Mickey Mantle, Yogi Berra, Derek Jeter, and Aaron Judge, who bring widescale attention from fans and the media.

Root, Root, Root for the Yankees

The Yankees are best known for their white and navy uniforms, with a fan-favorite uniform choice being the pinstripes. Their beloved ballpark is based in the Bronx in New York City, the birthplace of the team, where many of their loyal fans regularly attend their games during season. Select players are the face of this baseball franchise, but every member of the team has contributed to the Yankees legacy. This sport requires a great level of skill to be displayed throughout the game by the players in order to create such a widespread, loyal, and passionate fanbase, which the Yankees have accomplished in building.
