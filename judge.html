Aaron Judge: An All-Star Yankee

Early Life and Baseball Journey

Aaron Judge, better known as The Judge, was born on April 26th, 1992, in Linden, California, and adopted the following day. Judge attended Linden High School and participated in multiple sports, such as basketball, football, and baseball. He later was accepted to California State University, Fresno in 2010, and was drafted to the New York Yankees in 2013 within the first round as the 32nd pick.

Rookie Makes History

Judge made his debut with the Yankees in 2016 as a rightfielder, where he quickly became a reliable asset for the team both offensively and defensively. He was well-liked by fans after hitting a homerun during his first at bat in major league baseball. In 2017, just a year after his debut, Judge was awarded the Rookie of the Year Award due to hitting 52 homeruns as a rookie. Later in 2022, he received the Most Valuable Player Award in order to recognize his talent and achievenments.

Legacy in Leadership

He has been declared captain of the Yankees team since late 2022, and his uniform tends to represent #99. The Judge is known to have an incredible work ethic, devoting hours to specific practices he intends to improve, and arrives first to the field, which allows his teammates to follow in his example. He inspires a positive, motivational attitude within the team, and despite standing at 6 foot 7 inches, he is considered to be a "gentle giant." Aaron Judge has proved over many years to be a reliable asset to the Yankees franchise.
